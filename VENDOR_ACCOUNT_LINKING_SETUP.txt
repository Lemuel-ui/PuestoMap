PUESTOMAP - VENDOR ACCOUNT LINKING

No database migration is required for this feature. The vendors table already contains vendor_user_id and a foreign key to users.user_id.

HOW TO LINK AN EXISTING VENDOR ACCOUNT
1. Log in as BPLO Staff.
2. Open Vendor Management.
3. Find the business and click Edit.
4. In the Link Vendor Login Account panel, select the approved, active Vendor account belonging to that business owner.
5. Click Save Account Link.
6. The Vendor should log out and log in again, then open Business Profile or Dashboard.

FOR A NEW BUSINESS RECORD
1. Open Vendor Registration.
2. Complete the business details and pin its location.
3. In Link Approved Vendor Account (Optional), select the approved Vendor login if available.
4. Save Vendor Record.

NOTES
- Only approved and active accounts with role=vendor are selectable.
- An account already linked to another business is not offered for selection.
- One vendor login can be linked to one business record.
- If no account exists yet, leave the field as Link later / No account yet. Approve the Vendor's registration first, then link it through Edit Vendor.
- No existing business or user records are deleted by this feature.
